Life in Miraflores

Miraflores is one of Lima's best-known neighborhoods, and it's easy to live in.

  • The Malecón. A long path of parks along the cliffs above the Pacific. People walk, run and cycle here every day.
  • Parque Kennedy. The busy heart of the neighborhood, with cafés and restaurants all around.
  • Cafés and coworking. Plenty of places to work, meet or take a call.
  • Walkable. Many everyday things are a short walk away.
  • Safe. Miraflores is generally considered one of Lima's safer districts. Normal city sense still applies.

Questions

How long can I stay?

As long as it works for you. Stays are flexible, and there's no long lease. We use a simple one-page agreement.

How does payment work?

Rent is paid one month in advance. There's also a deposit equal to one month's rent. It's a standard way to keep things simple and fair for everyone in the house.

What if my plans change?

Just give me two weeks' notice before you leave.

Are meals included?

Breakfast and dinner are optional and home-cooked. Let me know what you'd like and we'll sort it out.

Do I need a visa?

In general, US citizens can visit Peru without a visa. Rules depend on your nationality and on why you're coming, for example to work or study. Please check your own situation with official sources before you travel.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. I'm from the US, so we can talk in English. Some Spanish helps in daily life in Lima, and Miraflores is a good place to start learning.

What's the neighborhood like?

Miraflores is walkable, generally safe, and close to the Malecón and Parque Kennedy. There are lots of cafés, restaurants and places to work nearby.
